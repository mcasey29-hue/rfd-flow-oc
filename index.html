Verified reviews & business protection

RFD FLOW protects customers’ right to honest feedback while also protecting businesses from fake reviews, review bombing and no-show complaints.

✓ VERIFIED CUSTOMER REVIEW

Proof before publication

A review is published only after RFD FLOW can reasonably verify that the reviewer had a real transaction, appointment, order or service interaction with the business.

⏳ PENDING CHECK

Negative is allowed, fake is not

Businesses cannot remove a review merely because it is critical. But reviews with no credible customer relationship, spam, threats, duplicates or fabricated claims are not published.

↩ BUSINESS RESPONSE

Dispute and reply

A listed business can report a review, submit evidence, request a re-check and post a public response. RFD FLOW keeps the review decision separate from paid membership.

🛡️ No-show protection
  • A person who never completed an appointment or transaction cannot post a service-quality review about an experience they did not receive.
  • A verified cancellation or no-show may be recorded privately for dispute handling, but RFD FLOW does not publish a public customer blacklist.
  • Businesses may publish their own cancellation, deposit and no-show policies on their profile.
  • If a customer paid a deposit or had a real pre-service interaction, they may review that specific interaction if it can be verified.

For verification, ask only for limited proof such as an order number, appointment confirmation or redacted receipt. Never request full card numbers, bank credentials, passwords, Social Security numbers or other unnecessary sensitive information.
